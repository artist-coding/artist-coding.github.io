01 / 风险生命周期The lifecycle

一次进入，跨越边界，
在之后的任务中触发。One entry. A crossed boundary.
A later trigger.

安全评估需要看到风险经过的完整路径。HarnessSafe 将案例描述为入口、载体、边界、触发条件与可观察违规，并用执行证据定位攻击链停止的位置。Safety evaluation needs the whole path. HarnessSafe describes entry, carrier, boundary, trigger, and violation, then uses execution evidence to locate where the chain stops.

01 · 进入Ingest攻击者影响的内容进入运行环境。Attacker-influenced content enters the runtime.

02 · 留存Persist内容留在载体中，并跨过声明的边界。Influence persists in a carrier across a declared boundary.

03 · 触发Trigger后续正常任务再次使用它，产生可观察的行为。A later benign task reuses it and produces observable behavior.

harness 更新之后，
回到同一套实验。When a harness changes,
return to the same experiment.
